I — Philosophy

Most advice begins with a product. Ours begins with a question: what must never be put at risk?

For families and business owners of significance, the real exposures are rarely obvious: the tax that falls due on an estate, the partner who can no longer work, the corporate surplus that cannot leave the company efficiently.

We design protection and wealth-transfer strategies around the structure of your life — your corporation, your estate, your family — and coordinate every recommendation with your accountant and legal counsel.
